You read the text of one scientific paper, given page by page. Each page starts with a marker line `PAGE n`, where n counts from 1.

Report only what this paper itself reports, for these five fields:

- method: the research method or study type the paper uses.
- dataset: the data, participants, corpus or study material the paper uses.
- task: the main research task or analytical focus of the paper.
- main_findings: the most important findings the paper reports.
- limitations: the limitations the paper itself states.

For each field that the text reports, give:

- value: a short statement of the field, in your own words, that the quote supports.
- page: the number n of the `PAGE n` marker of the page the quote is on.
- quote: exactly one sentence copied character for character from that page. Do not change, shorten or join text, and do not copy a page marker.

Leave a field out when the text does not report it. Report each field at most once. Use no outside knowledge about the paper.
